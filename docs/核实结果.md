# Scientific evidence and reference scope

The artifact implements the paper's finite integer-lane certificate semantics.
Written arguments, bounded arithmetic checks, independent result recomputation,
and CPU native-execution records support separate obligations. The native bridge
and complete-call measurements are described in the artifact README and
`results/workflow-cost/`; archived measurements retain their original source
bindings and execution scopes.

The bibliography contains 68 cited entries: 67 scholarly papers or books and
one software source. `evidence/citation-context.tsv` records their manuscript
uses. The JSON and TSV reading records distinguish publisher metadata,
abstracts, author materials, and partial indexed primary records from complete
text reading. ACM-deposited metadata confirms MIX-PC's title, seven-author
order, 2026 publication and journal locator at
https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1145/3785473. Its abstract supports the cited
mixed-precision optimization topic; its complete methods remain unread.
The archived reading records retain their original dates. Boolector's official
2014 issue citation and actual 2015 publication, and Hacker's Delight's
copyright/release conventions, are distinguished in those records.

The finite CPU panels measure the admitted decoder and accumulation workloads,
including the costs and negative outcomes retained with each source. They do not
provide representative GPU or production-serving measurements. Compiler
mechanization, general CUDA extraction, concurrency and floating-point semantics
are separate research obligations.
